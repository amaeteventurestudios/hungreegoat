Privacy Policy

Last updated 17 November 2026

HUNGREE Goat runs free, always-on music stations. This page explains what information is involved when you visit hungreegoat.com, listen on our player, or when our operator uses the HUNGREE Goat Control app with a YouTube account. Questions: info@hungreegoat.com.

Listeners and website visitors

  • You don't need an account to listen. We don't ask for your name, email, or any sign-in.
  • hungreegoat.com has no forms and loads no analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts, and sets no cookies.
  • The web player (player.hungreegoat.com) remembers your volume, chosen station, shuffle and time-of-day settings in your own browser's local storage. That stays on your device; clear your browser's site data to remove it.
  • Like any website, our web servers may record standard request details (such as IP address, time, and the page or stream requested) for security and troubleshooting. We don't use these to build profiles or sell them.

HUNGREE Goat Control and YouTube data

HUNGREE Goat Control is our private operator tool for running our own YouTube live stream. It is not offered to listeners or the public; only the HUNGREE Goat operator signs in to it with Google. It uses the YouTube Data API with one permission, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube, and uses it only to:

  • read the connected channel's name and ID, to show which account is connected;
  • list the channel's live streams and live broadcasts and read their status, to confirm YouTube is actually receiving and airing our stream;
  • move our own broadcast between testing, live and complete, only when the operator presses that button.

It does not upload, edit or delete videos, and does not read or change comments, subscribers, playlists or analytics.

What is stored, and where

  • The Google sign-in token is stored in a single file on HUNGREE Goat's own server, readable only by the account that runs the app.
  • The IDs of the bound YouTube stream, broadcast and channel are stored in the app's local database. The channel name and broadcast status are cached in memory for a short time.
  • Operational logs keep the most recent 20,000 events; stream-health measurements are deleted after 7 days.

Sharing

We don't sell, rent or share Google user data or listener information with anyone, and we don't use it for advertising or to train AI models. Google user data is used only for the purposes above. HUNGREE Goat Control's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Use of YouTube is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access and data

  • Pressing Disconnect in HUNGREE Goat Control deletes the stored Google token.
  • Access can also be revoked at any time from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • To ask about or request deletion of any other information, email info@hungreegoat.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
