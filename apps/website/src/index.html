About HUNGREE Goat

Afrobeats for the way
Africa lives today.

HUNGREE Goat is an independent 24/7 music broadcast. Our first station, Lo-Fi Afrobeats, plays original lo-fi reworks of Afrobeats, with warm rhythms and African textures made for studying, working, creating, and winding down. A second station, Afrobeats, is on its way.

The whole thing runs on our own broadcast system: a curated catalog, live scheduling, a resilient audio engine, and a player you can open anywhere without leaving for YouTube. Technology in service of culture, built to put African creativity on and keep it on.
